    About ITLA

    Awareness of terraced landscapes is growing considerably at the global, European, national and local levels. The most important civil initiative on the protection, preservation, and promotion of terraced landscapes and related cultures worldwide is the International Terraced Landscapes Alliance (ITLA). ITLA was formed in Mengzi, Yunnan Province in the Red River (Honghe) Prefecture in China in November 2010 by enthusiasts and visionaries who met there for the First World Conference on Terraced Landscapes. Since 2010 ITLA members have organised further conferences in Peru, Italy and the Canary Islands.

    ITLA members bring interdisciplinary expertise to the alliance's main objective of acting for the benefit of nature and the people who still live in terraced landscapes. The ITLA journals focus only on terraced landscapes and deal with these highly complex systems, run professionally and in accordance with international guidelines and publication ethics.

    Open Access is based on the view that unimpeded access to published information is essential for a fast and efficient flow of information. All publications are freely and universally accessible online, authors retain copyright on their works and all publications are stored in a digital Open Access archive.

    Join the alliance

    ITLA brings together researchers, farmers, masons, artists and local initiatives working for terraced landscapes and their people.
