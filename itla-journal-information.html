    Open access and costs

    International Terraced Landscapes Alliance Journal is a diamond open access journal: every contribution is free to read and download from the day of publication, and authors pay no submission or publication fees. From October 2026 contributions are published under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0). Contributions published before then remain under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0) under which they first appeared; the licence of each contribution is shown on its page and in its Zenodo record. Authors keep the copyright to their work.

    Identifiers, archiving and discovery

    • DOI: every contribution and every complete issue has its own DOI, registered with DataCite through Zenodo. Please cite contributions with their DOI.
    • Archive: the version of record (PDF and metadata) is deposited in Zenodo, the open repository operated by CERN, which keeps it for the long term. All ITLA publications are also gathered in the ITLA community on Zenodo.
    • Discovery: through Zenodo and DataCite the records are harvested into OpenAIRE, the European open science graph, and are findable in the EOSC (European Open Science Cloud).
    • Authors: ORCID iDs given by authors are recorded in the metadata and linked from the article pages.
    • Usage: view and download counts on the article pages come from Zenodo.

    Corrections

    Errors in a published contribution are corrected in its record on Zenodo, which keeps earlier versions, and then on this website. To report an error, use the Report an error button on any page or write to submit@itla.si.
