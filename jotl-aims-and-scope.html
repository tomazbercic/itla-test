    Aims and scope

    The Journal of Terraced Landscapes seeks articles related to terraced landscapes. All scientific contributions are subject to a double-blind peer review process.

    We invite authors to send original, previously unpublished papers in the following format:

    • Original research articles containing a clearly articulated research problem and proposing its development, consistent with the current call for papers and the general line of the journal. Volume: up to 5,000 words.
