    Instructions for authors

    Welcome to the instructions for authors page of  The Art of Terraced Landscapes Journal. These instructions are designed to help you prepare your manuscript for submission to our journal. We have provided detailed guidance on all aspects of the manuscript from formatting your paper to citing your references.

    We encourage you to read through these instructions carefully before you begin writing your manuscript. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are always happy to help.

    We invite authors to send papers in the following formats

    Short articles (containing a clearly articulated theme) – consistent with the general line of the journal.

    Volume: 1,000 characters.

    Important

    The editorial office accepts texts in English.

    The authors should ensure that their contributions meet acceptable standards of English language.

    The journal does not require any fees for publishing the article.

    The text and images are made available in accordance with the Open Access Gold formula and under the Creative Commons license Attribution-Share Alike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

    The authors are asked to make sure that the entries comply with the given instructions and format.

    The editorial office reserves the right to make certain modifications to the manuscript without affecting the content of the study. The editors reserve the right to reprint the text in anniversary and special issues.
