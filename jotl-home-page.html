    About the journal

    One of the oldest, but also most efficient, adaptations of humans to harsh living conditions is the terracing of steep mountain slopes in different climatic zones on all continents. Research and scientific studies on terraced landscapes have intensified in recent decades. This journal aims to contribute to these and focus on current, neglected and stimulating topics.
